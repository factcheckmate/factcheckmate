**Wind turbines kill approximately 1.17 million birds annually in the U.S., but this lacks context.**  
&nbsp;  
Cats kill 1-2.4 billion birds per year, buildings kill 600 million, vehicles kill 200 million, and fossil fuel projects kill 5.18 birds per gigawatt-hour compared to wind's 0.269 birds per gigawatt-hour. Climate change from fossil fuels - which threatens millions more birds through habitat loss and extreme weather - is a far greater threat. Solutions exist too: painting one turbine blade black reduces bird deaths by 70%, and AI systems can detect approaching birds and temporarily shut down turbines.
