**The data proves otherwise.**  
&nbsp;  
Between 2010-2020, Germany increased renewable generation by 149.5 TWh while simultaneously reducing fossil fuel generation by 130.9 TWh - and grid reliability improved. Germany's CO2 emissions from power generation dropped from 315 million tons to 185 million tons. Japan closed over 40 nuclear reactors after Fukushima without materially increasing fossil fuel use by offsetting with efficiency and renewables. European grid operators find they would need only 1-2 weeks of backup fuel for winter peaks, providing just 6% of output.
