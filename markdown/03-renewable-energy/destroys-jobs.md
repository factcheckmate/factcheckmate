**The renewable energy sector is creating millions of jobs.**  
&nbsp;  
While coal and fossil fuel jobs have declined, the U.S. energy and automotive sectors are seeing net gains of millions of jobs from embracing green technologies. Renewable energy projects are more labor-intensive than fossil fuel operations, creating more jobs per unit of energy produced. The most countries renewable energy sector employs significantly more workers than coal mining. Job losses in declining industries are real and require transition support, but framing it as "renewables destroying jobs" ignores the much larger job creation happening in clean energy manufacturing, installation, and maintenance.
