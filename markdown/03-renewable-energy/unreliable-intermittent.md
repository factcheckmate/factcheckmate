**Germany gets nearly 50% of electricity from renewables and has one of Europe's most reliable grids** - with average outages of just 0.25 hours per year compared to 1.28 hours in the U.S. South Australia has run its grid on 100% solar and wind for days at a time with no reliability issues. Modern grid management, diverse renewable portfolios, energy storage, and demand response easily handle variability - just as grids have always managed the intermittency of fossil fuel and nuclear plants that routinely shut down for maintenance, fuel issues, or safety concerns.
