Undocumented immigrants in the United States paid an estimated $89.8 billion in federal, state, and local taxes in 2023. Research indicates that immigrants, including undocumented immigrants, contribute more in taxes than they consume in government benefits, particularly over the long term. They pay income, property, and sales taxes while being ineligible for most federal benefit programs.
