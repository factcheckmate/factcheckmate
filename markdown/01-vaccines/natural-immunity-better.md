Vaccines provide immunity without the serious risks of disease. While natural infection can provide immunity, diseases like measles, polio, and pertussis can cause severe complications including brain damage, paralysis, and death. Vaccines train your immune system safely—you get protection without suffering through dangerous illnesses. For some diseases, vaccine-induced immunity is actually more reliable and longer-lasting than natural infection. Why risk permanent disability or death when you can get safe protection from a vaccine?
