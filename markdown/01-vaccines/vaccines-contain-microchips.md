This is completely false. No vaccines contain microchips or tracking devices of any kind—this is physically impossible. Microchips require power sources and are far too large to pass through vaccine needles. Vaccine ingredients are publicly listed and regulated. If governments wanted to track people, your smartphone already does that far more effectively than any imaginary microchip could. This myth has been thoroughly debunked by health authorities worldwide.
