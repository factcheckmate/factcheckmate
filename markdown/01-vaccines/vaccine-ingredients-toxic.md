The amounts of these substances in vaccines are extremely small and safe. Many vaccine ingredients occur naturally in your body, food, and environment - for example, you consume far more aluminum from food and formaldehyde is naturally produced in your body. Thimerosal (mercury-containing preservative) has been removed from nearly all vaccines as a precaution, though it was never shown to cause harm. All vaccine ingredients undergo rigorous safety testing by health authorities and internationally before approval.
