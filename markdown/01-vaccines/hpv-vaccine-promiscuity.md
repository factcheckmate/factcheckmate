No, multiple studies have shown that girls who are vaccinated against HPV are not more likely to engage in sexual activity at an earlier age.  
&nbsp;  
The HPV vaccine prevents cancer. Six types of cancer are caused by HPV, including cervical, throat, and anal cancers. Protecting children before they're exposed to HPV is crucial because the vaccine works best before any exposure. This is cancer prevention, not a statement about sexual behaviour. Countries with high HPV vaccination rates have seen dramatic reductions in HPV infections and pre-cancerous conditions without any increase in sexual activity.
