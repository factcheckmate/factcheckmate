Certain vaccines are not only safe during pregnancy but extremely important for protecting both mother and baby. Vaccines like flu, Tdap (whooping cough), and COVID-19 are recommended during pregnancy and provide critical protection. Vaccinating during pregnancy passes antibodies to the baby, protecting them in their first months of life. Some vaccines (like MMR) should be avoided during pregnancy, but can be given before or after. Always consult with healthcare providers about which vaccines are recommended during pregnancy.
