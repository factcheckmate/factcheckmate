Vaccine-preventable diseases are serious and potentially deadly—not harmless childhood rites of passage. Measles can cause brain inflammation, permanent brain damage, and death. Mumps can cause deafness and infertility. Rubella during pregnancy causes severe birth defects. Chickenpox can lead to serious infections, scarring, and shingles later in life. Before vaccines, these diseases killed or disabled thousands of children annually. Vaccines provide immunity without the risks of severe complications, hospitalization, or death.
