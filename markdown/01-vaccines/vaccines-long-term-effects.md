Vaccines are monitored for safety continuously and long-term effects are extremely rare. Most vaccine side effects occur within minutes to hours after vaccination—serious long-term effects are virtually non-existent. Vaccines have been used for decades with extensive safety records. The actual diseases prevented by vaccines cause far more long-term complications than vaccines ever could. Measles can cause brain damage years later, polio causes paralysis, HPV causes cancer. Health authorities continuously monitor vaccine safety through surveillance systems.
