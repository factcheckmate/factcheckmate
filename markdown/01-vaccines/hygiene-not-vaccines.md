While hygiene helps, vaccines are responsible for disease elimination. Historical data clearly shows disease rates plummeted immediately after vaccines were introduced, not gradually with sanitation improvements. Many diseases like measles, polio, and whooping cough spread regardless of cleanliness—they're airborne or spread through respiratory droplets. Countries with excellent sanitation still saw massive disease rates until vaccines were introduced. When vaccination rates drop, these diseases return even in modern, clean environments.
