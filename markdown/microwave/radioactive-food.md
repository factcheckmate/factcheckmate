Food cannot become radioactive from microwave cooking. Microwaves work by making water molecules vibrate to create heat - the energy is converted to heat and doesn't remain in the food. It's like saying a campfire makes food "fiery" - the heat source doesn't contaminate what it cooks. The FDA explicitly states that microwave energy "does not make food radioactive or contaminated." This is basic physics that health authorities worldwide confirm.
