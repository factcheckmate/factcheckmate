Microwaves use non-ionizing radiation that can't damage DNA or cause cancer. The FDA, WHO, and major cancer research organizations all confirm microwave ovens are safe when used properly. The radiation is the same type as radio waves, just at a different frequency - it lacks the energy needed to break chemical bonds or ionize atoms.
