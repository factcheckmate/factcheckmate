Microwaving actually preserves more nutrients than most cooking methods. Harvard Medical School and the FDA confirm that microwaves often retain more vitamins and minerals because they cook quickly without added water. Any cooking method causes some nutrient loss, but microwaving is among the gentlest. The shorter cooking times and minimal water use mean vegetables microwaved retain more vitamin C and folate than when boiled or roasted.
