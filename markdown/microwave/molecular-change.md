Microwaves don't create harmful molecular changes any more than conventional heating does. All cooking methods alter food molecules through heat - that's literally what cooking is. Microwaves simply heat water molecules through vibration, the same way conventional ovens heat through hot air. There's no scientific evidence of unique harmful molecular changes from microwave cooking that don't occur with other heating methods. Major health organizations worldwide confirm microwaved food is safe.
