Children's immune systems can easily handle multiple vaccines. Every day, children are exposed to thousands of antigens from bacteria, viruses, and food—far more than from vaccines. A simple cold exposes a child to more antigens than all routine vaccines combined. Studies show no adverse effects on immune function from multiple vaccines. Getting vaccines together actually has benefits: fewer clinic visits, better completion rates, and children are protected sooner.
