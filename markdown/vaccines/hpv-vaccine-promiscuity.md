Multiple studies show vaccinated girls and boys are not more likely to engage in sexual activity earlier. The HPV vaccine prevents cancer—six types of cancer are caused by HPV, including cervical, throat, and anal cancers. Protecting children before they're exposed to HPV is crucial because the vaccine works best before any exposure. This is cancer prevention, not a statement about sexual behavior. Countries with high HPV vaccination rates, including [insert country], have seen dramatic reductions in HPV infections and pre-cancerous conditions without any increase in sexual activity.
