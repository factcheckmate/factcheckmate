Diseases are rare precisely because of vaccination—stop vaccinating and they return. The bacteria and viruses that cause these diseases still exist globally and don't respect borders. When vaccination rates drop, outbreaks occur, as seen with recent measles resurgence in countries where rates declined. Unvaccinated travelers can bring diseases back to [insert country]. Community immunity only works when enough people are vaccinated. Stopping vaccination because diseases are rare is like removing your parachute mid-flight because you haven't hit the ground yet.
