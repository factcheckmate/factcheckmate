Most vaccines cannot cause shedding because they don't contain live virus. For the few vaccines that use weakened live viruses (like MMR), the virus is so weakened it can't cause disease in healthy people. In extremely rare cases with severely immunocompromised individuals, precautions might be needed, but this doesn't apply to the general population. You cannot catch a disease from being around vaccinated people—this is a dangerous myth. Healthcare workers in [insert country] are vaccinated precisely because vaccines prevent disease transmission.
