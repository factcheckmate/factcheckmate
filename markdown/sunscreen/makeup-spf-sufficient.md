**Cosmetic SPF is typically too low and insufficient.** Most makeup contains only SPF 5-15, while dermatologists recommend SPF30+. You also don't apply enough makeup to get the stated protection - sunscreen needs about 1 teaspoon for face alone. Unless your makeup specifically lists SPF50+, use additional dedicated sunscreen underneath for extended sun exposure. Remember to reapply sunscreen every 2 hours, not just apply once under morning makeup.
