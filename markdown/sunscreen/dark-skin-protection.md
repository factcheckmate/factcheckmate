**False.** While melanin provides some natural protection, dark-skinned people can still get sunburn, skin cancer, and photoaging from UV exposure. It just takes more sun exposure to cause damage. Melanin offers only small amounts of protection - not complete immunity. Everyone should use broad-spectrum SPF30+ sunscreen regardless of skin tone. Apply 30 minutes before sun exposure and reapply every 2 hours, especially after swimming or sweating.
