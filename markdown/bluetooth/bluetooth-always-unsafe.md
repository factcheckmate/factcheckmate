It's safe to keep Bluetooth on all the time. Bluetooth uses very low-power, non-ionizing radiation that operates within established safety limits set by health regulators worldwide. The radiation exposure is minimal - far less than what you get from cell towers, WiFi, or even natural background radiation. While turning off Bluetooth when not in use can save battery life, it's not necessary for health reasons. Modern Bluetooth technology (especially versions 4.0+) uses even lower power consumption than earlier versions.
