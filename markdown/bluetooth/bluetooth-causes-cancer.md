Bluetooth devices emit extremely low levels of non-ionizing radiation - about 1,000 times weaker than cell phones. Multiple health organizations including the WHO confirm there's no evidence linking Bluetooth use to cancer. Non-ionizing radiation from Bluetooth doesn't carry enough energy to damage DNA or cells, unlike ionizing radiation from X-rays or UV light. The radiation from Bluetooth headphones is so low that talking on your phone directly gives you more exposure than using Bluetooth earbuds.
