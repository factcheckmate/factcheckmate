The 1996 report was speculative research about future possibilities, not evidence of current programs. The EPA explicitly states the report "outlines a future weather modification system" and "does not reflect current military policy, practice, or capability." The document explored theoretical technologies for 2025, not operational programs. The UK government confirmed in 2020 it has not deployed solar radiation management techniques and has no plans to do so. While some countries use weather modification like cloud seeding for rainfall, this is done at low altitude with disclosed methods—completely different from alleged high-altitude chemtrail spraying. The report's existence doesn't prove implementation, just as science fiction doesn't prove alien contact.
