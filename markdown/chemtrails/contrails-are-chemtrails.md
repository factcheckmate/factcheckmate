Contrails persist for hours when atmospheric humidity is high - this is basic physics, not evidence of chemicals. In a landmark 2016 study, 76 of 77 atmospheric scientists (98.7%) confirmed they had never encountered evidence of a secret spraying program. Contrails form when hot water vapor from jet exhaust meets cold air at high altitudes and freezes into ice crystals. In humid conditions, these crystals remain and spread, resembling natural cirrus clouds. In dry air, they dissipate quickly. This variation is entirely explained by temperature, humidity, and altitude—not chemical content. The UK Met Office confirms persistent contrails have been observed since World War II, with the exact same formation mechanism.
