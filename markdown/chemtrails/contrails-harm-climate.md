Contrails do affect climate, but this is an unintended byproduct of aviation, not deliberate manipulation. Research shows contrail cirrus clouds can trap heat in the atmosphere, contributing to climate change—but this is an environmental concern about aviation emissions, not evidence of geoengineering. The UK government acknowledges contrails have climate effects but emphasizes these are unintended consequences being studied to reduce aviation's environmental impact. This is fundamentally different from the chemtrails conspiracy theory claiming deliberate chemical spraying for weather control. Legitimate geoengineering research exists but remains theoretical—the UK confirmed it has deployed no solar radiation management techniques. Conflating the real environmental impacts of aviation with conspiracy theories about secret spraying programs hinders actual climate action.
