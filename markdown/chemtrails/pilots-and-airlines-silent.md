A global conspiracy involving millions of aviation professionals with zero whistleblowers is logistically impossible. Consider the scale: tens of thousands of pilots, hundreds of thousands of mechanics, ground crew, air traffic controllers, aircraft manufacturers, fuel suppliers, and aviation regulators worldwide would need to be involved. Even NSA whistleblower Edward Snowden—who leaked classified information—stated chemtrails "are not a thing." Aircraft undergo rigorous inspections and maintenance with detailed documentation. Any modification for chemical spraying would require massive, visible equipment that mechanics would immediately notice. The economic impossibility alone is staggering—producing and secretly transporting millions of tons of chemicals would cost billions annually with no way to hide the logistics.
