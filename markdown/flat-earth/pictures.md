Thousands of photos and videos of a spherical Earth have been taken by satellites, space probes, and astronauts from multiple countries over many decades. We also have live video feeds from the International Space Station (ISS) showing the curvature of the Earth in real-time. To claim they are all fake requires an impossibly large global conspiracy spanning nations that are otherwise political adversaries.
