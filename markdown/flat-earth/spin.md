The Earth rotates at a constant, steady speed. We, and everything around us including the atmosphere, are moving along with it, so we don't feel the motion. This is the same principle as not feeling the speed of a car or airplane moving at a constant velocity. Gravity is more than strong enough to hold us firmly to the surface.
