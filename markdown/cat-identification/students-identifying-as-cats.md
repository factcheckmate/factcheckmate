No verified cases exist of students identifying as cats or schools accommodating such behavior. Furries are people with an interest in anthropomorphic animal characters - similar to Star Wars or comic book fans. They don't believe they ARE animals, they just enjoy the creative hobby. Academic researchers who study the furry community for over 15 years confirm furries have never requested litter boxes. This myth spreads through third-hand accounts without photos or evidence, despite every student having a camera phone.
