Completely false. This hoax has spread globally with claims from the USA, Australia (Cairns, Tasmania), UK (Banff Academy, Scotland), Canada, and New Zealand - every single named school has officially denied these allegations. NBC News investigated claims from at least 20 politicians and found all were untrue. Aberdeenshire Council (Scotland), Queensland Department of Education, Tasmanian officials, and dozens of US school superintendents have personally inspected facilities and confirmed no litter boxes exist. Politicians who made these claims often later retracted them after admitting they hadn't verified the information. The hoax includes variations like lowered cafeteria tables and bathroom accommodations, but none have any evidence despite every student having a camera phone. The myth conflates furries (a creative hobby fandom) with transgender students and is used to undermine LGBTQ+ protections.
