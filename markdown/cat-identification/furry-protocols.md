False. The 'furry protocol' claim originated from a single unverified email that was shared on conservative podcasts. The Waunakee School District superintendent in Wisconsin confirmed this was misinformation and no such protocols exist. In the UK, the Safer Schools organization investigated these claims and found no substantiated reports of such policies in any schools. These stories follow a pattern: they're based on anonymous tips, never include evidence, and are always denied by the named schools.
