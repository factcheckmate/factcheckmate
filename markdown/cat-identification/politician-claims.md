Politicians repeated unverified rumors, not facts. NBC News found at least 20 conservative politicians made these claims, but every named school district confirmed they were false. Many politicians later retracted their statements (like Nebraska Senator Bruce Bostelman) after admitting they hadn't verified the information. Republican Senate candidate Don Bolduc challenged schools to 'prove' litter boxes weren't there - the named school confirmed it was a false allegation. Politicians sharing unverified claims doesn't make them true.
