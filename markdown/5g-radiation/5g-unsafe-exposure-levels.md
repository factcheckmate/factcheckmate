Real-world measurements consistently show 5G operates far below safety limits. In the UK, Ofcom found maximum exposure levels of 1.5% of ICNIRP guidelines, with 5G-specific signals contributing just 0.039%. In Australia, ARPANSA measurements in Melbourne showed exposure levels less than 0.15% of safety limits—over 700 times below the threshold. The ACMA found that 97% of Australian 5G sites measured had EMF levels less than 1% of limits. These safety standards include substantial safety margins (50-100x) below levels known to cause any biological effects.
