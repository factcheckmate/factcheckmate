Higher frequency doesn't mean more dangerous—it actually means less penetration. 5G's millimeter wave frequencies (24-50 GHz) are mostly absorbed by the skin and don't penetrate deeply into body tissues like lower frequencies do. Safety guidelines specifically account for higher frequencies with additional restrictions. The main biological effect at any frequency is heating, and 5G power levels are far too low to cause harmful temperature increases. ICNIRP updated guidelines in 2020 to include stricter averaging areas for frequencies above 6 GHz. Research shows a 50W/m² mmWave signal (which wouldn't pass regulations) only raises skin temperature by 0.8°C—well below the 1°C safety threshold.
