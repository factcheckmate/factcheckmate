Extensive independent research exists from government agencies and international bodies with no financial ties to the telecommunications industry. ICNIRP is an independent scientific commission recognized by WHO, not funded by industry. National safety agencies like ARPANSA (Australia), PHE/UKHSA (UK), and FDA (US) conduct independent reviews. WHO's EMF Project coordinates research globally through government and academic institutions. The scientific consensus comes from systematic reviews of thousands of peer-reviewed studies by multiple independent expert groups. Many countries have government-funded research programs specifically examining electromagnetic field health effects. These organizations have statutory duties to protect public health, not industry profits.
