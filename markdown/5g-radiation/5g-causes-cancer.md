5G uses non-ionizing radiation, which doesn't have enough energy to damage DNA or cause cancer. Multiple health organizations including WHO, ICNIRP, FDA, and ARPANSA have confirmed that 5G networks operating within established safety guidelines pose no cancer risk. Unlike ionizing radiation (X-rays, UV), 5G radiofrequency waves fall into the same safe category as FM radio and Wi-Fi. Measurements show 5G exposure levels are typically less than 1% of safety limits—in the UK, Ofcom found maximum levels of just 1.5% of ICNIRP guidelines across tested sites.
