5G safety is backed by over 50 years of research into radiofrequency radiation. Organizations like ICNIRP reviewed extensive scientific literature before updating guidelines in 2020 to cover 5G frequencies. The WHO's International EMF Project has been assessing health effects since 1996. Regulatory bodies including ARPANSA, FCC, and Ofcom continuously monitor and review research. The scientific consensus from multiple independent expert groups worldwide confirms that 5G, when operated within guidelines, presents no established health risks. The frequencies used aren't new—many have been safely used for decades in other applications.
