Viruses cannot travel on radio waves or mobile networks—this is a biological impossibility. COVID-19 spread globally in areas with and without 5G coverage, including Iran which had no 5G infrastructure. The conspiracy theory linking 5G to COVID-19 has been thoroughly debunked by WHO, ITU, and health authorities worldwide. Research shows this misinformation spread rapidly on social media despite having zero scientific basis. There is no mechanism by which radiofrequency radiation could weaken immunity or interact with viruses.
