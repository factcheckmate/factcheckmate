**Multiple regions already operate on 100% renewables for extended periods.** South Australia has run its grid on just solar and wind for days with no coal, nuclear, or hydro backup. Low-carbon power (renewables plus nuclear) exceeded 40% of global electricity in 2024 for the first time since the 1940s. Studies show 100% renewable grids are feasible using diverse renewable sources, energy storage, demand flexibility, improved efficiency, and smart grid management. The U.S. has 200 gigawatts of cost-effective demand flexibility potential alone. It's a coordination and investment challenge, not a technological impossibility.
