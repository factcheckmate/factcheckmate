**Solar panels pay back their manufacturing energy in 1-4 years** depending on technology and location, then generate clean electricity for 25-30+ years. Wind turbines have similar energy payback periods of 3-6 months. Over their lifetime, these technologies produce 10-30 times more energy than was required to manufacture them. This myth ignores the reality that fossil fuel extraction, processing, and transportation requires continuous energy input throughout the fuel's lifecycle, while renewable infrastructure requires energy primarily upfront.
