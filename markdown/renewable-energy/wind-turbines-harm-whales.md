**NOAA scientists determined these claims are baseless.** There is no scientific evidence linking offshore wind farms to whale deaths. The coordinated disinformation campaign in New Jersey successfully reduced public support from 76% in 2019 to 54% in 2023 despite having zero scientific basis. Real threats to whales include vessel strikes, fishing gear entanglement, ocean noise from shipping traffic, and climate change impacts. By contrast, offshore oil and gas drilling routinely harms marine life through spills, underwater noise, and habitat disruption - yet receives far less scrutiny.
