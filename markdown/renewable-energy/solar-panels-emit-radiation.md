**This claim is entirely unsubstantiated.** Solar panels emit electromagnetic radiation at levels similar to a toaster oven - far below any threshold that could harm human health. Solar photovoltaic systems generate direct current electricity with minimal electromagnetic fields. Independent public and private research has found no connection between solar installations and reported health incidents. This myth is often spread by groups opposing renewable energy projects and has no basis in peer-reviewed scientific literature. If solar panels were dangerous, the millions of homes and businesses with rooftop solar worldwide would show evidence - they don't.
