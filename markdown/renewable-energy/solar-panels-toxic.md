**Solar panels can be easily and effectively recycled** - up to 95% of materials can be recovered and reused. Solar panels last 25-30 years, and recycling infrastructure is expanding as the first generation reaches end-of-life. Modern panels contain minimal hazardous materials, far less toxic than coal ash or oil spills. The "toxic waste" narrative ignores that fossil fuel extraction and combustion creates continuous toxic waste streams including mercury, arsenic, and radioactive materials that contaminate air, water, and soil permanently, while solar panel materials can be recovered and reused.
