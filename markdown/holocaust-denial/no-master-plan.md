The absence of a single comprehensive order doesn't negate the overwhelming evidence. The Nazi regime operated through verbal orders, euphemistic language, and distributed decision-making to maintain secrecy. However, we have extensive documentation including: Hitler's public threats against Jews, the Wannsee Conference protocols detailing extermination plans, Himmler's speeches explicitly referencing the annihilation program, thousands of orders for deportations and killings, and testimony from Nazi officials confirming the systematic nature of the genocide.
