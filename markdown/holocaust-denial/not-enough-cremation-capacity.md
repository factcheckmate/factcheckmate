This claim misunderstands multiple facts: First, not all victims were cremated in ovens—many were shot and buried in mass graves, or burned in open-air pits. Second, Nazi documents show the crematoria at Auschwitz-Birkenau had capacity to cremate thousands per day, far exceeding civilian crematorium standards because multiple bodies were burned simultaneously. Third, German companies designed and built these industrial-scale facilities specifically for mass murder. Engineering documents, correspondence between camp officials and crematorium manufacturers, and testimony from Sonderkommando prisoners who operated the facilities all confirm the capacity calculations.
