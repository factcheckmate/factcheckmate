Anne Frank's diary is authentic and has been verified through extensive forensic analysis by the Netherlands Institute for War Documentation. The handwriting has been confirmed as Anne's through comparison with her known writings and school papers. The ballpoint pen claim is false—forensic analysis found only two ballpoint pen marks on two loose pages that were not part of the original diary, likely added during later examinations. The diary itself was written in fountain pen and pencil, consistent with materials available during 1942-1944.
