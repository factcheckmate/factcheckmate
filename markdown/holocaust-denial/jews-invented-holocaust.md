This antisemitic conspiracy theory is contradicted by the fact that Nazi Germany itself documented the genocide extensively through its own records, created before any post-war considerations. The evidence comes from German sources including transport records, killing reports, camp administrative documents, and communications between Nazi officials. The Zionist movement and efforts to establish a Jewish homeland predated the Holocaust by decades. The idea that millions of people from different countries—survivors, liberators, Nazi perpetrators, and neutral observers—conspired to fabricate evidence is logically impossible and ignores overwhelming documentation from the perpetrators themselves.
