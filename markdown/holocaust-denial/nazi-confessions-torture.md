While some early post-war interrogations involved coercion, the vast majority of Nazi documentation and testimony was not obtained through torture. Many Nazi officials voluntarily provided detailed accounts, and their testimonies are corroborated by: extensive written records they themselves created during the war, physical evidence at camps and killing sites, thousands of survivor accounts, and demographic data. Even when torture claims are made, the confessions align with independent documentary evidence. The Holocaust is not proven by confessions alone but by convergent evidence from multiple independent sources.
