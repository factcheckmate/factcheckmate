Physical evidence of mass graves exists at all Operation Reinhard camps (Treblinka, Sobibor, and Belzec) despite Nazi efforts to destroy evidence. Archaeological investigations using ground-penetrating radar, core sampling, and excavations have identified mass graves, human remains, and personal effects. At Treblinka alone, investigations found human ashes, bone fragments, and teeth. The Nazis attempted to exhume and cremate bodies on open-air pyres in 1943 to hide evidence, but archaeological and forensic evidence still remains, confirmed by multiple scientific investigations.
