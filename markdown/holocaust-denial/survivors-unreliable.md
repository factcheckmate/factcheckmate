Minor inconsistencies in survivor testimonies are expected and normal - they reflect the traumatic nature of events, the passage of time, and individual perspectives, not fabrication. What's remarkable is the consistency across thousands of independent testimonies regarding core facts: the existence of killing centers, gas chambers, mass shootings, deportations, and systematic persecution. These testimonies are corroborated by Nazi documents, physical evidence, and perpetrator confessions. Historians use standard methods to evaluate testimony reliability, and Holocaust accounts meet rigorous evidentiary standards.
