The six million figure is supported by extensive Nazi documentation, demographic studies, and thousands of pages of records from concentration camps, ghettos, and killing operations. Nazi officials themselves meticulously documented deportations and deaths. Post-war demographic research comparing pre-war and post-war Jewish populations across Europe confirms approximately six million Jews were murdered. This figure is accepted by historians worldwide based on convergent evidence from multiple independent sources.
