Gas chambers are proven through multiple lines of evidence: German architectural plans and construction documents, Nazi correspondence discussing gas chamber operations, testimony from both survivors and Nazi perpetrators, physical remains of the chambers including chemical residue analysis showing Zyklon B traces, aerial photographs from Allied reconnaissance, and post-war investigations. The Leuchter Report, often cited by deniers, has been thoroughly discredited by chemists and engineers for its flawed methodology.
