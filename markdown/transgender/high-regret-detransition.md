**Regret rates for gender-affirming care are extremely low—less than 1%**.  
&nbsp;  
Which is lower than regret rates for hip replacements, obesity surgeries, and many other common medical procedures. Studies following thousands of transgender individuals show regret rates of only 0.3% for transgender men and 0.6% for transgender women who underwent surgical procedures. When people do detransition (which occurs in approximately 1-3% of cases), research shows the majority do so due to external pressures—lack of social support, discrimination, or financial barriers—rather than genuine regret about their gender identity. The myth of widespread regret is not supported by medical evidence.
