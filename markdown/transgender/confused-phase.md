**Research shows the vast majority of transgender youth maintain their gender identity long-term.**  
&nbsp;  
A 2022 study following transgender youth for five years after social transition found 94% continued to identify as transgender. The "it's just a phase" claim confuses normal childhood gender exploration (which all children engage in) with persistent gender identity. Medical protocols specifically distinguish between the two: healthcare professionals only support transition for youth who consistently, persistently, and insistently express a transgender identity over time. No one receives medical intervention based on a brief or inconsistent expression of gender questioning. The real risk isn't supporting transgender youth—it's denying them support, which research links to dramatically higher rates of depression, anxiety, and suicidality.
