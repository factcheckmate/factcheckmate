**Gender identity is not a choice—it's a deeply-held internal sense of self.**  
&nbsp;  
Just as cisgender people don't choose their gender identity, transgender people don't choose theirs either. Major psychological and medical organizations worldwide, including the American Psychological Association, Australian Psychological Society, and British Psychological Society, confirm that gender identity is a core aspect of who someone is, not a decision or preference. Research shows that attempts to change someone's gender identity (conversion therapy) are harmful and ineffective. What transgender people choose is whether to live authentically and pursue transition—but their underlying identity itself is not chosen. No one would voluntarily choose the discrimination, violence, and barriers to healthcare that transgender people face in [insert country].
