**Schools cannot and do not medically transition children—only healthcare providers can prescribe medical treatments, which require parental consent for minors.** What schools may do is respect a student's chosen name and pronouns, which is social support, not medical transition. Some schools allow students privacy about their gender identity due to safety concerns—many transgender youth face rejection or abuse at home. Schools must balance student safety with family involvement. No school is performing surgeries, prescribing hormones, or providing medical care. The claim often conflates social support (using a different name) with medical transition (hormones or surgery), which are completely different things. Parents concerned about their child's gender identity should focus on maintaining open communication and connecting with qualified healthcare providers if needed.
