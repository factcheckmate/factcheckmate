**The science on transgender athletes is far more complex than claimed, and current research doesn't support blanket bans.**  
&nbsp;  
Studies show testosterone suppression significantly reduces strength and athletic performance in transgender women, though the extent and timeline varies. Many sporting organizations require transgender women to maintain low testosterone levels for 1-2 years before competing. Importantly, there's enormous natural variation in physical characteristics among all athletes—height, lung capacity, limb length, muscle fiber composition. Elite sport has always involved people with unusual biological advantages. Notably, there's no evidence of transgender women dominating women's sports; if they had such overwhelming advantages, we'd see them consistently winning championships, which we don't. These policies should be based on emerging evidence and fairness for all athletes, not fear or prejudice.
