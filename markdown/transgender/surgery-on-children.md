**Gender-affirming surgeries on prepubescent children essentially never happen.** This is medical misinformation. The reality: young transgender children receive only social support (using chosen names/pronouns, wearing preferred clothing). Around puberty, some may receive fully reversible puberty blockers after extensive evaluation. In mid-to-late teens, some may access hormone therapy. Surgical procedures, when they occur at all in minors, are extremely rare, limited to chest surgery in older teenagers (typically 16+), and require extensive medical and psychological evaluation. Genital surgeries are not performed on minors. The process involves multiple healthcare providers, extended assessments, and informed consent—the same careful approach used for any serious medical intervention.
