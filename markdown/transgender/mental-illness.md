**Being transgender is not a mental illness.** Major medical organizations worldwide, including the WHO and American Psychiatric Association, confirm this. Gender dysphoria—the distress some trans people experience from the mismatch between their gender identity and assigned sex—is a diagnosable condition, but it's not the same as being transgender. The treatment for gender dysphoria is gender-affirming care, which helps align a person's body and social role with their identity. Research consistently shows that affirming someone's gender identity improves mental health outcomes, while attempts to change or deny their identity cause harm.
